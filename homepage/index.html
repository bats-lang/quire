Quire

Quire is an e-book reader for EPUB books. It runs in your web browser, where it can be installed as an app and used offline, and as an Android app.

Open Quire in your browser

What it does

  • Keeps a library of the EPUB books you import, with shelves, collections and search.
  • Reads them with pages you turn, a table of contents, search in the book, bookmarks, highlights and notes.
  • Lets you choose the type size, spacing, margins, font and theme, and can read a chapter aloud.
  • Gets books from catalogues you choose (OPDS), such as Project Gutenberg, and looks words up in dictionaries you import.
  • Can keep your places, highlights, notes and reading time the same on your devices, through a storage service you choose.

Your data

Your books and your reading data are kept on your device. See the privacy policy for what is stored, what leaves your device and when, and how Google user data is used if you sync with Google Drive.
